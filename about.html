🐶 About Kelly Page

Kelly Page started as a console for one Tait radio on the P25X network in Queensland, and grew into a toolbox for hams: LoRa mesh, APRS, Bluetooth, SCADA trainers and a GPS tracker you build yourself.

Free software for hams

Kelly Page is free, and released under the MIT licence: use it, change it, share it. It's made for licensed amateur radio operators, and anything it sends to the APRS network goes under your own callsign.

The P25X network

P25X links Tait P25 repeaters across Queensland over the internet. Kelly's radio pages (texts, bulletins, the PBBS, SSTV, cameras, weather and updates over the air) talk to the P25X gateway. Everything else (LoRa mesh, the APRS iGate, Mesh Buddy, BLE Presence, the demos) works for any ham.

Privacy

This site has no tracking, no cookies and no ads. Kelly Page keeps its settings in your own browser and talks only to your radio, your LoRa node, your own bridge and (if you turn them on) the P25X gateway and APRS-IS.
