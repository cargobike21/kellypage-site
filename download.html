📻 On the P25X network? Everything for your own radio, LoRa mesh (MeshCore, Meshtastic), the APRS iGate and Bluetooth works straight away. The P25X gateway's services (weather, files, the PBBS, SSTV, updates over the air and the rest) need the station registered: in Kelly, Admin › Register this station, with your callsign and radio ID. Once it's approved, Kelly collects its registration by itself.

On a PC

  1. Unzip it somewhere you'll find it again, for example Documents\Kelly Page.
  2. Open index.html in Chrome or Edge. (Firefox and Safari can't talk to USB devices.)
  3. Plug in your gear by USB: a Tait radio's programming / data lead, or a MeshCore / Meshtastic node. Kelly's Admin page connects to it.
  4. Bookmark it. That's it. 🎉

Want to use the radio from another computer, or keep Kelly running with the PC off? Kelly comes with a small bridge program (start-kelly.bat, Python 3) for IP, and the Raspberry Pi image does all of it for you.

Updating

  1. In your old Kelly: Admin › Save config.
  2. Unzip the new version over the old folder.
  3. Open Kelly and press Ctrl+F5 once.
  4. Anything missing? Admin › Load config.

Or let Kelly do it: Admin › Kelly updates › Get it from kellypage.app downloads the new version, checks it and installs it into your Kelly folder. Registered P25X stations can also get updates over P25 data, overnight.

Checking the download

The SHA-256 above should match the file you downloaded. In PowerShell:

Get-FileHash .\KellyPage-*.zip
