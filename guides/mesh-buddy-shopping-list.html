Putting the GPS in the right slot

The RAK12500 goes in slot C.

The RAK19003 has two module slots, C and D. RAKwireless rates the RAK12500 for slot A or C, and Meshtastic's RAK19003 instructions use slot C. Slot D is the one people report problems with. In the wrong slot the GPS may show up but never get a fix. Each slot is labelled on the board, and the module is held by one small screw.

You'll also need

  • A 3.7 V single-cell LiPo battery with a JST PH 2.0 mm plug. That's the battery socket on the RAK19003, and no kit includes a battery. Mesh Buddy charges it over USB-C. Choose the size for how long you ride: 1000-2000 mAh is a sensible start. Use a rechargeable Li-ion or LiPo only; RAK says the battery must never exceed 4.3 V.
  • A weatherproof case that lets the GPS see the sky. Mount the LoRa antenna upright.
  • A computer with Chrome or Edge to flash it from Kelly's LoRa Mesh > Mesh Buddy tab. The guide walks you through it.
⚠️ Check the battery polarity before you plug it in.

LiPo batteries are sold with their JST plugs wired both ways round. Compare the red and black wires with the + and − marks beside the battery socket. A reversed battery can destroy the board. If they don't match, get a battery that does.

No on/off switch.

The RAK19003 has no power switch. To turn the tracker off, unplug the battery. If you want a switch, see the upgrade below.

Should you add the motion sensor?

Not yet. The usual WisBlock motion sensor is the RAK1904 (ST LIS3DH accelerometer). IoT Store has it for $13.20; I couldn't find it at Core Electronics.

Mesh Buddy doesn't read a motion sensor today. It tells moving from parked by GPS speed, which already sets the beacon rate (smart beaconing, at least 2 minutes apart while moving) and the telemetry rate (every 10 minutes moving, every 30 parked). A motion sensor would only help once the firmware uses it to turn the GPS off while you're parked, for longer battery life. When that happens, a RAK1904 fits the RAK19003's free slot D with one screw, so nothing is lost by waiting.
